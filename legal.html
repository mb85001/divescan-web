Terms of Use

For the DiveScan app

The service

DiveScan lets you scan pages of a paper dive log, uses an AI vision model to extract the data, and stores the result in a digital logbook synced through your own private iCloud account. New installs receive a number of free scan credits; additional credits can be purchased as in-app purchases through the Apple App Store.

Purchases

All purchases (scan credit packs) are processed by Apple via the App Store. Pricing, billing, and refunds for in-app purchases are governed by Apple's own terms and policies. Scan credits do not expire but have no cash value and are non-transferable.

Accuracy of extraction

DiveScan uses an AI model to read handwritten and printed dive log pages. While extraction is generally accurate, it is not guaranteed to be error-free — that's why every scan goes through a review screen before anything is saved. You are responsible for checking extracted data before confirming a save.

The App is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind, to the extent permitted by applicable law. We aim for high availability of the extraction service but cannot guarantee uninterrupted access, since it depends on third-party infrastructure (Apple, Anthropic).

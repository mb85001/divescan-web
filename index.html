How it works

From soggy logbook page to searchable dive.

Four steps, no retyping, and nothing saves without you confirming it.

SURFACE

Capture

Scan a page with your camera, or import an existing photo. Handles PADI logs today — more formats are just a template away.

2
AT DEPTH

Extract

Claude's vision model reads every field — dive number, site, depth, times, gas mix, even your handwritten notes.

Features

Built for logbooks, not spreadsheets.

Everything about DiveScan is designed around one page of handwriting and the dive behind it.

AI that reads handwriting

A vision model extracts every field from a scanned page — from dive number to marine life notes — and flags anything it isn't sure about.

Private by design

Your dive data lives in your own private iCloud database. Our servers only ever see how many scan credits you have left — never your dives.

Built for any logbook

Ships with PADI's format out of the box. New templates plug in without touching the scanning or review pipeline.

Two dives, one page

Some logbooks print two dives per sheet. DiveScan detects both and walks you through reviewing and saving each one.

Never lose a dive

Scans made offline queue automatically and extract the moment you're back online. Nothing saves without your confirmation.

Export whenever you want

Take your logbook with you: UDDF for dive-planning software, CSV for spreadsheets — no lock-in, ever.
